# Henzen — project record

Status: working prototype, **not an MVP**, not a commercial product. Lead and solo-founder: Federico Nebuloni.

## Problem and goal

Explore which information about hens in a small flock could be useful to their keeper, and make that information readable.

## What I personally built

- Physical prototype enclosure and integration, including manual fabrication and laser cutting.
- ESP32 firmware in C++, developed by me with AI coding tools as support.
- Integration of RFID, ESP32, data transfer to Firebase, and a web dashboard.
- Tests with hens; the dashboard displays data collected by the real system.

## Validation and limitations

I interviewed prospective users, received initial expressions of willingness to try and buy the product, and spoke with three University of Milan academics interested in testing the prototype. These are early signals, not sales, completed university tests or completed market validation. A broader system for the chicken coop is still under development.
