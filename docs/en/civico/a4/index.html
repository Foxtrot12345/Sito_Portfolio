Initiative · Ossona

A4: from concern to proposal

I began with a question about motorway noise near Ossona, then turned it into an article and a formal observation, bringing the issue toward public discussion.

Federico Nebuloni · 2025–2026

Starting point

People living near the A4 motorway in Ossona experience its noise. I decided to examine the issue and look for public processes through which to raise it with the municipality.

The work

  1. 2025
    Article. I wrote “Ossona senza difese”, published by CO Notizie, to explain the problem and suggest possible next steps.
  2. NEXT
    Formal observation. I submitted an observation during the municipality's noise-classification planning process.
  3. 2026
    Public meeting announced. A meeting was organised to present the work and open questions about A4 noise.

Why this belongs here

This work differs from Henzen but follows a similar logic: observe a problem, study it, communicate it and seek a concrete route toward a solution. I do not claim that mitigation measures have already been installed on the motorway.
