About me

My path has taken different directions, but one thing connects them: I like to understand how things work and take responsibility for moving them forward.

My Life

Technology is more than my field of work: it is the lens through which I explore the world. Curiosity, discovery, and a constant desire to leave a positive mark wherever I go shape my path.

My Vision

I see the world's rapid evolution primarily as an opportunity, even as it brings risks. I face the future with optimism, convinced that continuous improvement and lifelong learning are the keys to my growth.

My Goal

I want to build projects that make a real difference in people's lives, beyond simply carrying out assigned tasks. My goal is to bring together independence, the freedom to explore the world, and the ability to create genuine value.
