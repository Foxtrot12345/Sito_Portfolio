Henzen

From observing a real problem to a system that collects data from hens and makes it readable. This is what I have built so far — and what I am still trying to validate.

Federico Nebuloni · Solo-founder · In development

How it began

Henzen grew from a wish to make it easier to observe what happens in a small flock. I took the idea off paper and built an initial system to test with real hens.

The journey

  1. 01 / IDEA
    A question to test. Which data would actually help someone looking after hens?
  2. 02 / BUILD
    A working prototype. I developed ESP32 firmware in C++ and connected RFID identification, Firebase and a dashboard. AI coding tools supported my development work.
  3. 03 / TEST
    Real data, not just screens. The system has been tested with hens; the dashboard shows information collected by the prototype.
  4. 04 / NEXT
    From test to product direction. I am exploring a smart nest and a broader experience that still need development and validation.

My contribution

As solo-founder, I have led the idea, firmware development and prototype construction, alongside conversations with prospective users and external contacts. The technical work was also a practical learning journey: I learned to read and modify C++ as I built.

What exists today? A prototype tested with hens and a dashboard fed by real data. I do not present it as an MVP or a product already on sale.

What I am validating

I have heard initial willingness to try and buy the system, and spoken with University of Milan academics in animal science who expressed interest in testing the prototype. These are promising early signals, not completed external tests or sales.

Current direction

The project is moving toward a broader system for the chicken coop. The Henzen website explains the product vision. This page keeps that vision separate from what the prototype has already demonstrated.
