# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People evaluating Federico Nebuloni's work and background, including potential employers, collaborators, and readers who want more context than a CV provides. Public text resources also support AI-agent reading.

## Product Purpose

A public, bilingual portfolio that presents Federico's projects, practical work, civic initiatives, education, and interests with enough context for a visitor to explore the underlying work.

## Operating Context

The site is a static, multi-page HTML/CSS/JavaScript project published from `docs/` on GitHub Pages at `https://fedenebu.github.io/`. Italian and English versions have separate routes. Public Markdown profiles and `llms.txt` are available as text resources but are not linked in the human navigation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The existing site has pages for the personal profile, links, projects, Henzen, and civic work.
- The Globe is a dedicated navigation page with a large, rotatable, photographic Earth and a starfield, closely following the composition of Simone Mattioli's Globe page. Italy, Spain, and Switzerland are highlighted in green as **countries in focus**, not a claim of visits. No location pins or personal travel stories are shown.
- The site must remain usable on narrow screens and without exposing the local CV or phone number.
- Avoid presenting a prototype as an MVP or inventing outcomes, travel history, or associations not supplied by Federico.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing project and civic pages in `docs/`.
- A public portrait at `docs/assets/federico-nebuloni-portrait.png`.
- Simone Mattioli's public Globe page is the visual reference for the Globe's composition, not a source of claims about Federico.

## Product Principles

- Show concrete work and clear provenance rather than inflated claims.
- Keep Italian and English versions in step.
- Make geographic highlights descriptive without implying unsupported personal history.
